# AbangBus Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 24, 2026

AbangBus uses account information to authenticate riders and approved drivers. Riders may save favorite stops. During an active driver trip, the app collects precise device location, speed, heading, accuracy, mock-location status, and timestamps so riders can see the current bus position.

Current bus positions are removed when a trip ends. Detailed driver position history is retained for up to 30 days for safety, incident investigation, service reliability, and abuse prevention. Application security audit records are retained for up to 365 days. Account and favorite-stop data remain until the account or saved item is deleted, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Location history is not sold or used for advertising. It is available only to authorized operational systems and personnel. Public riders receive only the current active-trip position required for live tracking.

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of account data through the operator contact published with the production release. Requests may require identity verification. Some records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal compliance.

Data is hosted using Supabase infrastructure. Authentication providers such as Google process information under their own privacy terms when users choose those services.

Before public launch, the operator must replace this paragraph with its legal business name, physical or mailing address, privacy contact email, and any jurisdiction-specific disclosures required for operating in the Philippines.
